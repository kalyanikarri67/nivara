Real Life Scenario
Scenario A: A Normal Active Morning

User greets caregiver, requests water, confirms breakfast and morning medicine. Routine stays normal.

Scenario B: Unresponsive / Quiet Morning

No greeting, no water, and no medicine confirmation by 11:00 AM. NIVARA does not panic with loud false alarms. Instead, it alerts the caregiver hub that 3 routine steps are missing so someone can check on them.
